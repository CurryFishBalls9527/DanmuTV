Everything you'd expect, done natively

Not a web wrapper: playback uses tvOS's own AVPlayer, and every screen is designed around focus and the Siri Remote.

Native 4K / HDR playback

4K / 8K, HEVC / AV1, HDR10 / HLG, Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos and FLAC; dynamic range and frame-rate matching; quality follows your bandwidth.

Smooth danmaku, person masking

Off-main-thread rendering keeps dense bullet comments smooth. Tune size, speed, area, density and filters (synced with your account's block list); with Bilibili's masks, comments pass behind people.

Cast from your phone

Tap Cast in the official Bilibili app and pick Danmu TV. The TV opens the same video in its own player, with danmaku, history and quality; pause, seek and speed follow the phone.

Danmaku heat bar

A curve of how busy the comments are, drawn above the scrubber, with the liveliest moments marked so you can jump straight to them.

Interactive videos, picture in picture

Choose story branches with the remote and step back when you like; shrink the video to a corner and keep browsing.

Anime, live, comments

Anime and series with schedules, followed shows and OP / ED skipping; live streams with danmaku and Super Chat; comment threads with images and emotes.

Made for the remote

Hold the clickpad for a temporary 2× / 3×, autoplay of the next part and related videos, and Continue Watching on the Top Shelf.

Accounts, and no tracking

QR-code login, up to 5 accounts synced across Apple TVs through iCloud Keychain. No analytics, no crash reporting.

How to install

Danmu TV isn't on the App Store and no prebuilt packages exist: you build it from source with your own Mac and Apple ID, onto your own Apple TV.

  1. Get ready

    A Mac with Xcode 26 or later, brew install xcodegen, and an Apple TV (tvOS 17+) on the same network.

  2. Clone and set up signing

    Copy Config/Local.xcconfig.example to Local.xcconfig and fill in your Team ID and a bundle ID of your own.

  3. Pair the Apple TV

    On the Apple TV open Settings › Remotes and Devices › Remote App and Devices, then pair it in Xcode's Devices window.

  4. Run

    Pick the DanmuTV scheme and your Apple TV and press ⌘R. A free Apple ID's signature lasts 7 days; a paid developer account's lasts a year.

git clone https://github.com/CurryFishBalls9527/DanmuTV.git
cd DanmuTV
cp Config/Local.xcconfig.example Config/Local.xcconfig
xcodegen generate
open DanmuTV.xcodeproj

FAQ

Is Danmu TV made by Bilibili?

No. It's an independent open-source project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Bilibili (Shanghai Kuanyu Digital Technology Co., Ltd.).

Does it cost anything? Is it on the App Store?

It's free and GPL-3.0 licensed. It has never been published on the App Store, TestFlight or anywhere else, and no packages are provided; anyone charging for it is not us.

Can I install a build someone else made?

Please don't. Danmu TV signs in to your Bilibili account, and a build made by someone else could be tampered with. Only install what you built yourself from this repository.

Can I watch premium (大会员) content?

Only with your own premium account. Danmu TV doesn't bypass any paywall, membership, region or copyright restriction, and doesn't break any DRM.

Which Apple TVs are supported?

Apple TV HD and Apple TV 4K on tvOS 17 or later. 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision need an Apple TV 4K and a TV that supports them.

Is the interface in English?

Not yet: the interface is Simplified Chinese only.
