## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-11

First public release.

### Added
- Browsing: recommended (with stage), popular, rankings, following feed, live, anime & series with schedule and
  followed series, search, history, watch later, favorites, followings; card long-press menu.
- Player: DASH→HLS with adaptive quality; 4K / 8K, HEVC / AV1, HDR10 / HLG, Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos / FLAC;
  display matching; CDN strategies; resume, continuous play, related autoplay; hold for 2× / 3×; loop, mirror,
  chapters, subtitles; OP / ED and sponsor-segment skipping; debug panel.
- Danmaku: off-main-thread rendering, filtering, merging, account block-list sync, person masking.
- Hot comments with replies and likes.
- Live: HLS and FLV (VLC), live danmaku and Super Chat.
- Casting from the official Bilibili phone app (UPnP + NirvanaControl and NVA).
- QR login, up to 5 accounts synced via iCloud Keychain; Top Shelf with continue watching; SMB playback;
  alternate app icons.
